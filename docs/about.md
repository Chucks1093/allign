# Align

**Align is a web app that makes tokenized stocks on Base simple for everyone.**

Stocks are now available as onchain assets on Base. Align gives users one clean place to discover, buy, manage, gift, and automate those stocks — without needing to understand blockchain.

---

## The problem

Tokenized stocks exist on Base, but using them today requires understanding wallets, DEXs, token standards, and blockchain transactions. That's a barrier most people won't cross.

---

## What Align does

Align removes that barrier. The user speaks in plain language:

> "Buy $25 of Nvidia every Friday."

> "Send my friend $50 of Apple."

> "Tell me when Nvidia drops more than 5%."

Align handles everything underneath.

---

## Core features

**Explore** — Browse tokenized stocks available on Base. See prices, performance, and buy in one click.

**Portfolio** — See everything you own in one place. Total value, individual holdings, performance.

**Buy & Sell** — Simple flow. Pick a stock, enter an amount, confirm. No blockchain jargon.

**Gift** — Send a tokenized stock to any eligible wallet. Add an optional message. First of its kind.

**AI Manager** — Talk to the AI in plain language. It understands what you want, builds a strategy, and asks for your approval before doing anything.

**Automation** — Set rules like "buy $25 of Nvidia every Friday" or "buy more if it drops 5%." Align runs them within the limits you define.

**Rewards** — Earn tokenized stocks for referrals and community activity.

**Activity** — Full history of every buy, sell, gift, and automated action. Every transaction links to Base.

---

## Who it's for

- People who know stocks but are new to crypto
- Crypto users who already have USDC and a Base wallet
- Active investors who want to monitor multiple tokenized stocks
- Users who want AI to help manage a simple investment strategy
- Businesses who want to reward customers with tokenized stocks

---

## The big idea

Base made stocks programmable. Align makes that programmability useful to normal people.

The user should think: *"I'm managing my stocks."* — not *"I'm interacting with smart contracts."*
